Area

The habitable equatorial band of Orcus ranges from 10° north to 10° south, an area of approximately 163,000 square miles, approximately that of California and twice that of Guyana. This band is 95 miles from top to bottom and 1715 miles long. A "river" 1-3 miles wide bisects the equator, which, along with various megastructures, take up 5% of this surface, reducing its habitable territory to 155,425 square miles. All of these are approximations.

Coordinates

The equatorial band can be divided into 6504 squares of 5 miles per side which are laid out in staggered strips, functionally identical to hexagons. That band is therefore 1715 squares wide and 19 squares high. Coordinates are rendered in a simple (n,-n) format, with (0,0) being a specific city near the river.

These squares can be further subdivided into 25 squares of 1 square mile each. Coordinates here are expressed with letters in a (nn) format if needed.


Climate Bands

Equatorial Orcus is the only part of the planet which is habitable. The environment encircles the planet in perfectly rings, a product of the "solar system" which emits light from a single ring suspended over the equator by great arches.

Mangroves and swampy terrain blanket the land up to ten miles (two squares) from the coast, quickly thinning out to grassy plains for another fifteen (three squares) before becoming cold and dessicated as the land rises and the air becomes thinner (three squares). The terrain beyond 10° north or south, or 45 miles from the equator, is impassable without protective gear.
