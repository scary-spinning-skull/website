The hull of the Warden is not solid, but honeycombed with many tunnels and chambers. A common type of tunnel is the service corridor, an X-shaped configuration of tunnels which were intended to offer access to lengthy components of the ship which can't be moved. Each tunnel is 20' high and wide, and the floors, walls, and ceilings are covered with a bewildering array of wires, pipes, and boxes covered with blinking lights. There is almost always a 5' wide catwalk suspended from the ceiling with steel cables, and anchor points for engineers' grapples are scattered across every other surface seemingly at random.

These corridors connect to each other as they run through the hull, sometimes opening back onto a deck, and, rarely, offering characters a way to access the ship's exterior. To determine where and at what angle, simply roll on the table below for every five minutes of game time spent walking.
